# Gophle Momo Business Intelligence & Growth Strategy Analysis

## Overview

Gophle Momo is a data-driven business analytics project designed to evaluate the feasibility, profitability, and long-term growth potential of a Nepali food startup operating through a phased expansion strategy.

The project uses business intelligence, financial analysis, market research, customer analytics, and competitor benchmarking to identify opportunities for sustainable growth in a competitive food service industry.

Rather than focusing solely on sales performance, this project evaluates the entire business ecosystem, including startup investment requirements, operational costs, menu profitability, customer behavior, marketing effectiveness, competitor positioning, and future expansion opportunities.

## Business Objective

The primary goal of this project is to answer the following question:

**How can Gophle Momo successfully enter the market, achieve profitability, and scale into a long-term food destination while minimizing financial risk?**

The project explores:

* Startup investment planning
* Food truck launch strategy
* Land lease and development planning
* Outdoor dining expansion
* Vendor leasing opportunities
* Customer acquisition strategies
* Marketing effectiveness
* Menu engineering and profitability
* Long-term business growth

---

## Business Model

### Phase 1: Mobile Food Truck Launch

The business begins with a mobile food trailer to establish brand awareness, generate revenue, and test market demand while minimizing startup costs.

### Phase 2: Land Development

A leased property will be developed into an outdoor dining area with covered seating and customer gathering spaces.

### Phase 3: Food Truck Park

Additional food vendors will be invited to lease spaces on the property, creating multiple revenue streams and increasing customer traffic.

### Phase 4: Permanent Facility

Long-term plans include a permanent restaurant, commercial kitchen, catering services, and expanded operations.

## Data Sources

This project utilizes multiple datasets to simulate and analyze business operations.

### Sales Data

Tracks transactions, revenue, profit, customer types, and purchasing behavior.

### Customer Data

Analyzes customer demographics, preferences, satisfaction, and purchasing patterns.

### Competitor Data

Compares pricing, ratings, reviews, strengths, and weaknesses of competing businesses.

### Marketing Data

Measures advertising performance, customer acquisition, and return on investment across marketing channels.
### Menu Profitability Data

Evaluates food cost, selling price, profit margin, and menu optimization opportunities.
### Expense Data

Tracks monthly operating expenses and fixed business costs.
### Investment Data

Estimates startup capital requirements for each business phase.
### Sales Scenario Data

Projects revenue under various customer demand scenarios.
### Vendor Leasing Data

Estimates future rental income from food truck vendors operating on the property.
